injustice is when you can't look away

even when you look away, it doesn't go away

even if you try to walk away, it hounds you

try to think happy thoughts, hope against hope and still no good

'cos it catches all of it in a dreamcatcher deathgrip

all that's left is this moment, and the next and the one after that

the moments till one way or another you can't breathe

injustice is now

all around you
