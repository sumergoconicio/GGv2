![](/archive/media/57dbbed6f3e3a1db3b78.png)

thank you dear teacher

who i’ve never once met

for your reaching me

by your simply being you

now you are gone far away

and i feel closer to you than ever

you have given me the joy of nothing

i will treasure it forever
