like a photograph is a celebration of captive light

with just a click of the the shutter, a moment in time is frozen

your defence too is the culmination of illuminated discovery

within the span of a day, a new title has been garnered

three cheers to your success young doctor

celebrate tonight for you've certainly earned your merit

but just as the many coincidences that must've come together in a lucky photograph

don't forget the hours of fascination and frustration that led you to this very moment

for the joy of science is forever in the seeking

not just what we occasionally find glinting back in the light
