what will the collapse look like?

will the ground finally give way

and devour us in its cosmic maw?

more likely it will come unannounced

and remain hidden, we men none the wiser.

convinced that our predations of each other

are part of yet another grand master scheme

as opposed to the ignorant tantrums of fools

unable to find peace or show grace

kicking screaming and clawing will we go

every step of the way to oblivion

why one might even say

we’ve never stopped falling

from Eden.
